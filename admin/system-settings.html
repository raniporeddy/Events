Backend Integration Details:

EVENTS includes a production-grade Node.js & Express backend (server.js) with Nodemailer for direct SMTP delivery.

  • OTP Length: 6 Random Digits
  • OTP Validity: 5 Minutes (300 seconds)
  • Resend Cooldown: 45 Seconds
  • Password Security: Bcrypt Salt Hashing (10 rounds)
  • Development Mode: Active with on-screen modal fallback

SMTP Configuration in .env:

SMTP_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
SMTP_PORT=587
SMTP_USER=your-university-notifications@gmail.com
SMTP_PASS=your-google-app-password
SMTP_FROM="EVENTS <no-reply@events.edu>"
