Verify Certificate Ask Support
How do I register for an event?

Simply navigate to the Events catalog, choose any event, and click "Register for Event". If you are signed in, your student details are auto-filled, and a QR ticket pass is generated immediately.

How do digital participation certificates work?

When you attend an event and the organizer scans your digital QR pass at the entrance desk, attendance is recorded automatically. Your official certificate is instantly unlocked under your profile and verifiable on our public verification portal.

Can faculty members and student clubs propose events?

Yes! Faculty members and recognized student clubs can submit event proposals with custom schedules, capacity limits, and venue preferences through the Event Proposal page or faculty portal.

How can companies post campus recruitment drives?

Corporate recruiters can register under the Company role and access the dedicated Industry Partner Portal to post placement drives, track applicant resumes, and export attendee rosters.
